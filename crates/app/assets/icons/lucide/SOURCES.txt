Lucide 1.63.0 (https://lucide.dev, https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide), ISC (LICENSE, beside
this file). The files here are the icons MDM draws from, as Lucide publishes them (lucide-static
1.63.0); crates/app/src/icons.rs and browsericons.rs carry their path data.

- check, x, plus, square, trash, copy, file, folder, folder-open, settings, hand, move, arrow-down,
  circle-arrow-down, circle-stop, alarm-clock, alarm-clock-check: the toolbar and the dialogs.
- globe: a browser MDM has no logo for.
- circle-check, circle-x, triangle-alert, circle-ellipsis: a result's mark (it holds, it failed, a
  warning, under way): Test browser integration, "Installed", "Connected".
- circle-check, circle-arrow-down, circle-pause, clock, circle-x, circle-arrow-up, circle-ellipsis:
  each download's status icon (complete, downloading, paused, queued, failed, seeding, checking)
  where the theme has none of its own.
- images: gallery-dl's role icon in the Helpers window (gallery-dl has no logo of its own).
- circle-arrow-down, rss, key-round, globe, files, link: an empty list's role icon (the download
  list, RSS feeds, Accounts, Site rules, a queue's files, Link grabber); Add torrent's is the
  toolbar's magnet.
