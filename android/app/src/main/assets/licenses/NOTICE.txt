Mister Download Manager for Android

Mister Download Manager is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3
(GPL-3.0); its full text follows below. Its source, this app's included:
https://github.com/anefzaoui/misterdownloadmanager

The app carries these programs and files by others. Each is a separate program MDM runs, or a
file it shows, under its own licence; where a licence asks for the source, it is at the address
given.

Python 3.12 (runs yt-dlp and gallery-dl)
  Python Software Foundation License 2.0, with the licences of the libraries it comes with
  (OpenSSL: Apache-2.0; libffi, expat: MIT; zlib: zlib; SQLite: public domain).
  Built for Android by the Termux project and packaged by youtubedl-android 0.18.1 (GPL-3.0).
  Source: https://github.com/termux/termux-packages (python and its dependencies),
  https://github.com/JunkFood02/youtubedl-android (tag 0.18.1)

ffmpeg and ffprobe 7.1 (joins a video's picture and sound)
  GPL-3.0-or-later as built (--enable-gpl --enable-version3, nothing non-free), with the
  libraries it uses under their own licences (x264, x265, Xvid: GPL-2.0-or-later; libvpx, libaom,
  dav1d, Opus, Vorbis, Theora, libwebp: BSD; LAME: LGPL-2.0-or-later; GnuTLS: LGPL-2.1-or-later;
  and others listed by the Termux package).
  Built for Android by the Termux project and packaged by youtubedl-android 0.18.1.
  Source: https://github.com/termux/termux-packages (ffmpeg and its dependencies),
  https://github.com/JunkFood02/youtubedl-android (tag 0.18.1)

QuickJS (runs the JavaScript some video sites need)
  MIT. Packaged by youtubedl-android 0.18.1.
  Source: https://github.com/JunkFood02/youtubedl-android (tag 0.18.1), https://bellard.org/quickjs/

7-Zip 26.06 (extracts 7z and RAR archives), by Igor Pavlov
  GNU LGPL 2.1 or later, with BSD 3-clause and BSD 2-clause parts and, for its RAR code, the
  unRAR licence restriction; its full licence follows below. Built from its source for Android
  by this app's build (android/app/build.gradle.kts).
  Source: https://github.com/ip7z/7zip/releases/tag/26.06 (7z2606-src.tar.xz, SHA-256
  a4e374c8f864f51df48c2cfd0e1413ec3b2ea030deae852d6a655402ebb61ac4)

Archivo (the app's typeface), by Omnibus-Type
  Copyright 2020 The Archivo Project Authors (https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo).
  SIL Open Font License 1.1; its full text follows below.

DB-IP IP to Country Lite (which country a download's server is in, looked up on the phone)
  IP Geolocation by DB-IP (https://db-ip.com). Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Country flags: the phone's own emoji.

yt-dlp and gallery-dl are not in the app: MDM downloads them from their own releases when you
first need them, and checks them against their developers' signatures. yt-dlp: The Unlicense
(public domain), https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp. gallery-dl: GPL-2.0, https://github.com/mikf/gallery-dl.

The app's Rust and Android libraries, and their licences, are listed in the source: Cargo.lock
and android/app/build.gradle.kts; ATTRIBUTION.md lists the assets.
