# Website: misterdownloadmanager.com

Scope: every page `tools/website/build.py` makes. Home, download, IDM alternative and
benchmark are Persuade; help, FAQ, news and legal pages are Read and take the same world with
restraint. 21 languages, Arabic and Persian right to left; light and dark.

Visitor: an IDM user, often non-technical, looking for a free replacement that works the same.
Action: Download (Windows installer from GitHub Releases). Proof: the real app window, the
public benchmark, the price of nothing. Constraint: clean room, no IDM artwork; no invented
claims; every image is the real app.

Memorable moment: the real MDM window as the parcel, with its waybill stuck on it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A download is a delivery. The site is the waybill of a file: what is in the parcel (the
window IDM users know), how fast it arrives (the benchmark, as tracking), what the postage is
(nothing). It refuses the category default: dark hero, glowing screenshot, rows of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: The logo's blue as the board at page scale (the user's choice, replacing kraft
after seeing it), white text on it; thermal-label white sheets with pure black ink and hairline
field rules; condensed grotesk caps for field names, a plain grotesk for reading, tabular figures
for every number; a real, scannable Code 128 barcode of the release. Palette law: the logo's green
is reserved for the one action (Download) and delivered states; blue is the board and, on labels,
links. Dark: a navy depot, reverse-print labels (near-black stock, cool white ink) on deep navy. Raises, by donor: fixed field ranking, service /
routing headline / fine print on every label (pulp rack); one word per section at
architectural scale, the delivery time (street poster); green by law only for action and
delivered (arcade palette law); every tracking event shows its rate as well as its time, motion
damped (night instruments); colour lives in fields and stamps, reading text stays ink
(cloud edge); the tracking route is one continuous rule from first scan to delivered (neon).

STORY: The visitor recognizes IDM's window before reading a word, reads that it is rebuilt,
faster and free, sees the delivery times against IDM, and downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The real main window, about 84% of the content width and set toward the end,
as the parcel. A waybill label card, about a third of the width, is stuck across its lower start
corner (the owner's choice, after trying the end corner): tilted about two degrees, its depth in
a contact shadow and a long soft one (no 3D turn, no pointer movement: the owner's call), sharing
the window's grid cell so the section clears it. It carries the sender block (logo,
name), the headline set large, the subline, the green Download action, macOS and Linux with their
icons, the fields (postage free; delivery time; for Windows 10 and 11; version and size) and the
barcode. A slim header above. Mobile: the window full width, the card below overlapping its lower
edge with a gentler tilt, the caption under it.

FORM: grounded candidate 5 of 7 (courier waybill and parcel label); seed:d1b9f806aa0c2dc4.
Signature interaction: the tracking race, two shipments scanned event by event to the real
benchmark times, replayable. Motion grammar: the label feeds out once like a thermal printer;
progress moves damped; nothing else animates on entry.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
