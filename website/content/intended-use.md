MDM downloads what you point it at, the way a web browser does, only faster and with more control. It's made for everyday downloads: software and updates, documents, your own files from cloud storage, videos and music you're allowed to save, Linux images and other torrents shared by their makers, and anything else you have the right to download.

- **No content of its own.** MDM doesn't host, index, search for or recommend anything to download. What it downloads comes from the addresses you give it.
- **No site-specific shortcuts around rules.** MDM treats sites the same way. It has no features made for getting around a particular site's terms, paywall or limits.
- **No breaking of copy protection.** MDM doesn't remove or get around DRM, and isn't meant to. Protected content stays protected.
- **Your downloads are yours to answer for.** Respect copyright and the terms of the sites you download from. With torrents, remember that a torrent client also shares what it downloads.

The full terms are in the [Terms of use](/terms.html).
