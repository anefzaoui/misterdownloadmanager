The free alternative to Internet Download Manager

Mister Download Manager keeps the layout IDM users know, brings your IDM list over, and costs nothing: free and open source, with no trial, no ads and no sign-up. Below it, nine other free download managers people use instead of IDM, compared honestly.

Compare the alternatives

In short

If you want Internet Download Manager (IDM)'s layout without its price, the free alternative is Mister Download Manager (MDM): a free, open-source download manager for Windows 10 and 11, with macOS and Linux builds to try. It looks and works like IDM, imports your IDM download list, downloaded a 1 GiB file 5.4 times faster than IDM 6.42 in our published benchmark, and downloads torrents, magnet links and videos too.

Other free options: Free Download Manager, AB Download Manager, Motrix, Neat Download Manager, Prism, JDownloader 2, Xtreme Download Manager, Gopeed and DOWNitUP. See how they compare.

Coming from IDM?

Here's what stays the same, what's better, and what IDM still does that MDM doesn't.

Why IDM users switch

  • It's free, for good. No trial that runs out, no licence key, no ads. The GNU GPL guarantees it stays free.
  • Nothing to relearn. The toolbar, categories, download list, progress window, queues, Scheduler and Options sit where you expect them. Even the command-line switches are the same: /d /p /f /a /n /s.
  • Faster where it counts. In our benchmark against IDM 6.42 on the same computer and server, MDM downloaded 1 GiB in 1.0 s against 5.5 s, and a small file in 0.03 s against 3.1 s. See every run.
  • Torrents and magnet links. In the same list, with a file tree to pick from. IDM's website doesn't mention BitTorrent.
  • 86 languages built in. Right to left included, chosen on the first start.
  • Open source. Anyone can read the code, check what it does and build it. No adware, no telemetry.
  • Linux and Mac too. Builds with every release, if you use more than Windows. From version 0.5.0, Linux is no longer experimental: it is checked on GNOME, KDE Plasma, Xfce and Cinnamon.

Switch in five minutes

  1. In IDM, choose Tasks → Export → To IDM export file and save your list.
  2. Download Mister Download Manager and install it; keep Set up browser integration ticked.
  3. In MDM, choose Tasks → Import → From IDM export file and pick the file. Your downloads arrive with their addresses, referrers and cookies.
  4. Add the MDM extension to your browser (how) and turn IDM's off, so only one of them takes your downloads.

What IDM still does that MDM doesn't

We'd rather you knew. IDM has a site grabber that follows a whole website's pages (MDM's link grabber takes the links of one page), and many years of polish and compatibility fixes. MDM is new; if something you rely on is missing, ask for it.

Questions

What is the best free alternative to IDM?

If you want Internet Download Manager's layout without its price, Mister Download Manager: free and open source, laid out like IDM, and it imports your IDM list. If you need something else first, such as an Android app or a particular feature, the comparison above shows what each of the others is good at.

Is there an open-source IDM?

IDM itself is closed source. Mister Download Manager is open source (GPL-3.0) and laid out the way IDM users expect, but it's a separate program written from scratch. Anyone can read its code on GitHub.

Is it related to IDM?

No. Mister Download Manager is an independent project written from scratch in Rust. It has no connection to Tonec Inc. and contains none of IDM's code, artwork or text. Internet Download Manager and IDM are trademarks of their owner, named here only to compare. More

Is it really free, or a trial?

Really free, with no time limit, paid edition, ads or sign-up. More

Can I keep IDM installed too?

Yes. They don't share settings or files. Turn off one browser extension so each download goes to one program.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

Yes: every release has builds for Linux and macOS. Today's are marked experimental; from version 0.5.0, Linux isn't, after checks on four desktops, and macOS stays experimental. Windows 10 and 11 are the main platform. More
