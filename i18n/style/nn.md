- Use the imperative for commands and action buttons. Menu items that perform an action use the same form; do not use infinitives as labels. (Opne; Lagre)
- Phrase check boxes as concise statements describing what the setting does. Use sentence case and omit a final full stop. (Start nedlastingar automatisk)
- Address the user as «du» and use a friendly, direct, neutral register. Avoid overly formal or ceremonious wording. (Du kan endre dette seinare.)
- Write confirmation prompts as direct questions, normally with «Vil du …?». Name the action or item clearly. (Vil du avbryte nedlastinga?)
- Keep labels and column titles short, usually as noun phrases without articles. Avoid adding prepositions or explanatory wording unless needed for clarity. (Nedlastingsmappe; Filnamn)
- Use a present-tense verb for an ongoing activity and a concise noun phrase or full clause for a status. Use an ellipsis only when the activity is still in progress. (Kontrollerer …; Nedlastinga pågår.)
- Make count-based status text grammatical without relying on a single form that may not agree with the number. Prefer a plural noun phrase with a verb that agrees. ({n} nedlastingar pågår)
- Turn English error fragments into a natural Nynorsk message. Keep the cause or error detail after a colon, and preserve placeholders exactly. (Kan ikkje opne fila: {error}.)
- Write event-log entries as clear, concise statements, usually in the past tense. Include the item or action where it helps identify the event. (Nedlastinga vart fullført.)
- Use sentence case: capitalise the first word and proper names, but not every word in a title. Write months and weekdays in lowercase. (Innstillingar for nedlasting; måndag 12. juni)
- Do not add a full stop to a button or a short label. Use a full stop for a complete statement and a question mark for a question. (Start; Nedlastinga er fullført.)
- Use a single space between words and after punctuation, but none before commas, full stops, colons or question marks. Use the ellipsis character with a space before it. (Kontrollerer …; Feil: prøv på nytt.)
- Use Norwegian angle quotation marks for quoted text. Do not put interface labels in quotation marks unless referring to their exact wording. (Meldinga «Fila finst ikkje» vart vist.)
- When the subject is understood from context, use a natural verb form for that subject; do not copy an English fragment if it produces awkward or incomplete Nynorsk. (Lastar ned …; Fila er klar.)
- Use natural Nynorsk word order in full sentences, with the finite verb in the usual second position. Do not mirror English word order. (Når nedlastinga er ferdig, opnar fila seg.)
- Prefer established Nynorsk words to English loanwords. Use a loanword only when it is established in the product domain or is part of a name, and use Nynorsk spelling and inflection. (lenkje; innstillingar)
