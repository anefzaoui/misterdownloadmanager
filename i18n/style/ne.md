- Use polite, direct instructions. Address the user as तपाईं when needed; usually omit the pronoun and use the respectful imperative ending गर्नुहोस्. (डाउनलोड सुरु गर्न डाउनलोड गर्नुहोस्।; फाइल चयन गर्नुहोस्।)
- Phrase action buttons as short, polite imperative verbs. Keep the action first and omit explanatory wording that is not needed to identify it. (पज गर्नुहोस्; फेरि प्रयास गर्नुहोस्)
- Use imperative verbs for action menu items; use concise noun phrases for menus that open a view or category. (फाइल खोल्नुहोस्; डाउनलोड इतिहास)
- Phrase check boxes as concise statements of the option’s effect. Do not turn them into questions. (डाउनलोड पूरा भएपछि सूचना देखाउनुहोस्)
- Ask confirmation questions politely and state the action clearly. Use के … चाहनुहुन्छ? where appropriate. (के तपाईं यो फाइल मेटाउन चाहनुहुन्छ?)
- Use short noun phrases for labels and column titles. Nepali has no articles; omit them, and omit postpositions when the meaning remains clear. (फाइल नाम; स्थिति)
- Use the shortest natural form for progress and status. Preserve placeholders exactly, and use a classifier and plural agreement for counted items. (जाँच गर्दै…; {n} वटा डाउनलोड भइरहेका छन्।)
- Use impersonal, concise wording for errors. Keep the error detail after a colon and preserve its placeholder. (खोल्न सकिएन: {error})
- Write event-log entries as brief statements about what happened, without addressing the user. (डाउनलोड सुरु भयो।; फाइल सुरक्षित गरियो।)
- Nepali script has no uppercase or lowercase. Use sentence-style wording for titles rather than imitating English Title Case. (डाउनलोड सेटिङहरू; फाइल जानकारी)
- Use the approved localized names for weekdays and months; do not apply English capitalization rules. Follow the Windows locale’s date format. (आइतबार; जनवरी)
- Use Nepali punctuation consistently: no space before punctuation and one space after it. Use … for an ellipsis and avoid repeated dots. (जाँच गर्दै…; डाउनलोड पूरा भयो।)
- Use standard word spacing, including around placeholders. Do not insert spaces inside a word or separate a punctuation mark from the preceding text. (त्रुटि: {error}; २ वटा फाइल)
- Use quotation marks only when the interface needs to identify literal text. Use Nepali-compatible curly quotation marks and preserve quoted content exactly. (“फाइल खोल्नुहोस्” चयन गर्नुहोस्।)
- Prefer neutral constructions that avoid an unnecessary subject. Make the verb agree naturally with an expressed subject or count. (डाउनलोड पूरा भयो।; ३ वटा फाइल डाउनलोड भइरहेका छन्।)
- Use natural Nepali word order: place modifiers before the noun and put the verb at the end of a sentence. (नयाँ डाउनलोड गरिएको फाइल; यो फाइल खोल्न सकिएन।)
- Use familiar, established technology loanwords in Devanagari rather than forcing uncommon literal translations. Keep product and brand names unchanged. (फाइल; फोल्डर)
