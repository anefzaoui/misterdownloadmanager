- For commands, buttons, and menu actions, use the Slovak infinitive. Keep the action concise; do not switch to an imperative just because the control is a button. (Stiahnuť; Pozastaviť sťahovanie)
- Phrase check box labels as short statements or infinitive phrases that clearly describe the enabled behavior. Make the checked and unchecked meaning unambiguous. (Spúšťať aplikáciu pri prihlasovaní; Po dokončení sťahovania vypnúť počítač)
- Address the user formally with Vy and its grammatical forms. Prefer neutral instructions and infinitive action labels over conversational or overly deferential wording. (Zadajte adresu URL.; Vaše nastavenia sa uložili.)
- Write confirmation prompts as direct, polite questions. State the consequence when it is important; use a question mark. (Naozaj chcete odstrániť tento súbor?; Chcete po dokončení sťahovania vypnúť počítač?)
- Use concise noun phrases for labels and column titles. Omit articles, and use prepositions only when needed for clarity. Prefer a clear noun over a sentence. (Cieľový priečinok; Názov súboru)
- Use an ellipsis for an operation that is still in progress, and a concise status phrase for a completed or current state. Keep variable text grammatically independent where possible. (Kontroluje sa…; Sťahuje sa…)
- For counts that change, use correctly localized Slovak plural forms or restructure the text to avoid attaching a fixed noun form to a number. (1 súbor, 2 súbory, 5 súborov; Počet aktívnych sťahovaní: {n})
- Write error messages as complete, natural Slovak phrases where space permits. For a reusable error fragment, put the cause or detail after a colon. (Nepodarilo sa otvoriť: {error}; Súbor sa nepodarilo stiahnuť.)
- Write event log entries as brief, factual statements. Use past tense for completed events and include the relevant object when it helps identify what happened. (Sťahovanie súboru bolo dokončené.; Pripojenie k serveru sa nepodarilo.)
- Use sentence-style capitalization: capitalize the first word and proper names, not every word in a title. Write Slovak month and weekday names in lowercase. (Nastavenia sťahovania; pondelok, január)
- Use a period for complete statements and a question mark for questions. Do not add a final period to short labels, buttons, or headings. Use an ellipsis character for a continuing operation. (Sťahovanie sa dokončilo.; Zrušiť)
- Follow Slovak spacing and typography. Do not put a space before commas, periods, colons, semicolons, or question marks; use a space after them. Use Slovak quotation marks. (Chyba: súbor sa nenašiel.; Zadajte hodnotu „1“.)
- Make status fragments agree with the implied noun, or include the noun to make the agreement clear. Do not leave a gendered adjective without context. (Sťahovanie pozastavené; Súbor stiahnutý)

- Use the same Slovak term for the same concept throughout the interface, including menus, notifications, help text, and logs. Do not vary wording merely to avoid repetition. (Sťahovanie; Pozastaviť sťahovanie)
