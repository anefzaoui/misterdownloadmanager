- Commands and action buttons use a respectful imperative; do not add “कृपया” unless the source explicitly expresses politeness. Menu commands use the same form, usually with the object first. (डाउनलोड करें; फ़ाइल खोलें)
- Checkbox labels describe an option or state rather than addressing the user. Use a concise noun phrase or infinitive. (डाउनलोड पूरा होने पर सूचना दिखाना; Windows के साथ प्रारंभ करें)
- Address the user as “आप” and use respectful verb forms. Avoid “तुम” and conversational or overly formal honorifics. (क्या आप डाउनलोड हटाना चाहते हैं?; आपकी सेटिंग्स सहेजी गईं)
- Phrase confirmation prompts as direct, complete questions. Use “क्या आप…”; use “वाकई” only when the source stresses confirmation. (क्या आप इस डाउनलोड को हटाना चाहते हैं?; क्या आप वाकई सभी डाउनलोड रोकना चाहते हैं?)
- Labels and column headings are short, usually noun phrases. Omit articles and prepositions where Hindi remains clear; do not turn headings into sentences. (फ़ाइल नाम; डाउनलोड स्थिति)
- Keep progress and status text concise and natural. Use an ellipsis for an ongoing action; preserve placeholders and make number agreement clear. (जाँच जारी है…; {n} डाउनलोड हो रहे हैं)
- Translate error fragments as natural Hindi, retaining the colon and error placeholder. Include an explicit noun when needed to make gender agreement clear. (फ़ाइल नहीं खोली जा सकी: {error}; डाउनलोड पूरा नहीं हो सका: {error})
- Event log entries are brief, factual statements, generally in the past tense. Omit an understood subject when the event is clear from context. (डाउनलोड शुरू किया गया।; फ़ाइल सहेजी गई।)
- Devanagari has no uppercase or lowercase. Write Hindi titles in normal sentence-style wording; preserve the official capitalization of English product names and acronyms. (डाउनलोड सेटिंग्स; Microsoft Edge)
- Write Hindi month and weekday names in lowercase-style Devanagari, without English-style title capitalization. (सोमवार; जनवरी)
- Use Hindi danda (।) to end complete Hindi sentences and a comma (,) for a pause within a sentence. Do not add final punctuation to short labels or headings. (डाउनलोड पूरा हुआ।; नाम, स्थिति)
- Do not put a space before punctuation; use one space after a comma or colon when text follows. Keep punctuation attached to its preceding word. (त्रुटि: फ़ाइल नहीं मिली; नाम, स्थिति)
- Use double quotation marks (“ ”) only when quoting literal text or a UI label. Do not use quotation marks merely for emphasis. (“डाउनलोड” चुनें; फ़ाइल का नाम “setup.exe” है)
- When the subject is omitted, make the verb agree with the implied Hindi noun, not with the user. If agreement could be unclear, name the subject. (डाउनलोड पूरा हुआ।; फ़ाइल डाउनलोड हो गई।)
- Prefer natural Hindi word order, generally placing the object before the verb. Keep variables next to the phrase they modify, without changing their spelling or braces. (फ़ाइल डाउनलोड करें; {n} फ़ाइलें डाउनलोड हो रही हैं)
- Use established Hindi UI loanwords and write them consistently in Devanagari. Prefer familiar terms over literal or unfamiliar translations; use nukta in standard forms such as “फ़ाइल”. (फ़ाइल; फ़ोल्डर)
- Distinguish pausing from stopping and resuming: a pause is temporary; stopping ends the current action. (डाउनलोड रोकें (अस्थायी रूप से); डाउनलोड बंद करें)
- Use “स्थिति” or a clear noun phrase for a status, not an imperative. Avoid adding a subject or explanation that the source does not contain. (स्थिति: डाउनलोड हो रहा है; तैयार)
- Keep UI terminology consistent across screens, menus, notifications, and logs; do not alternate between a loanword and a synonym for the same feature. (सेटिंग्स; डाउनलोड रोकें)
