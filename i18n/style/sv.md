- Use the informal second person, du, when addressing the user. Avoid Ni and unnecessary pronouns; use a natural imperative for instructions. (Välj en mapp.; Du kan ändra inställningen senare.)
- Phrase commands and action buttons as concise imperatives. Keep the same verb for the same action throughout the app. (Spara; Pausa)
- Use established menu names for categories and imperative verbs for actions in menus. (Arkiv; Redigera)
- Phrase check boxes as short descriptions of what enabling the option does. Avoid questions and labels that only say Ja or Nej. (Starta hämtningen automatiskt; Visa meddelanden när en hämtning är klar)
- Ask confirmation questions directly and specify the action or item. Make the buttons describe the choices. (Vill du ta bort den här hämtningen?; Ta bort)
- Keep labels and column titles concise, usually as noun phrases without articles. Use prepositions when they make a label clearer; do not force labels into full sentences. (Filnamn; Storlek)
- Write progress and status text naturally in Swedish. Use an inflected verb or passive form, and retain placeholders exactly. (Kontrollerar...; {n} filer hämtas)
- Make error messages clear and grammatical rather than copying English fragments. Keep the error detail placeholder in its intended position. (Det gick inte att öppna: {error}; Kunde inte ansluta till servern.)
- Write event log entries as concise, complete statements, usually in the past tense. Include terminal punctuation when the entry is a sentence. (Hämtningen startades.; Anslutningen till servern misslyckades.)
- Use sentence case for titles, headings, labels, and buttons: capitalize the first word and proper names, not every main word. (Inställningar för hämtning; Lägg till länk)
- Write Swedish month and weekday names in lowercase, except at the start of a sentence. (måndag 14 juni; Nästa hämtning: fredag)
- Follow Swedish punctuation and spacing: no space before punctuation, one space after it, and no spaces around a slash used as a separator. Use three periods for an ongoing-action ellipsis, with no space before them. (Kontrollerar...; Fel: {error})
- Use Swedish quotation marks when quoting text. Preserve punctuation inside the quotation marks only when it belongs to the quoted text. (Filen ”rapport.pdf” finns redan.; Meddelandet visar ”Åtkomst nekad”. )
- Check agreement with the implied subject. Swedish imperatives omit the subject; when a status could be unclear or require agreement, name the item or use a neutral verb form. (Välj en mapp.; Hämtningen är slutförd.)
- Use natural Swedish word order, including verb-second order in main clauses. Put subordinate-clause verbs at the end. (Välj den mapp där filerna ska sparas.; När hämtningen är klar öppnas filen.)
- Prefer established Swedish terms over English loanwords. Use a loanword only when it is established in Swedish or is a product or technical name; do not invent Swedish spellings of English verbs. (ladda ned; ladda upp)
- Distinguish stopping an operation from cancelling it. Use the term that matches the actual effect. (Stoppa hämtningen; Avbryt dialogrutan)
- Use consistent terminology for downloading: ladda ned for the action and hämtning for the process or item. (Ladda ned filen; Hämtningen är klar)
